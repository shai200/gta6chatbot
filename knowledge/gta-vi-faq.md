# GTA VI launch FAQ (unofficial fan helper knowledge base)

Written in our own words from public, confirmed facts. Checked 20 Nov 2026.
Each fact carries a source tag; URLs are listed at the bottom. Nothing here is copied
guide text. Rumours are either excluded or explicitly labelled RUMOUR.
This is a fan project. It is NOT affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

## Release and platforms
- F1. Grand Theft Auto VI came out on 19 November 2026 (a Thursday). [S1][S2][S3]
- F2. It is out on PlayStation 5 (including PS5 Pro) and Xbox Series X and Series S. [S1][S4][S5]
- F3. It is not playable natively on PS4 or Xbox One. [S4][S5]
- F4. Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming, which needs cloud playtime (included monthly with Game Pass, or bought separately). Cloud streaming uses Series S graphics settings. [S5][S6]
- F5. PC: Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a PC release date. A first-quarter-2027 PC window has been reported by media citing anonymous sources: that is a RUMOUR, not confirmed. [S1][S7]
- F6. The game is not on Xbox Game Pass; you buy it separately. Game Pass members can use their monthly cloud hours to stream a copy they own. [S5]
- F7. Rockstar's page says the game "plays best" on PS5. [S1][S8]

## Editions, price, pre-order extras
- F8. Standard Edition: US$79.99 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, digital, or a boxed copy that contains a download code. [S2][S9]
- F9. Ultimate Edition: US$99.99, digital only. It adds extra in-game items: premium vehicles, weapons, outfits, tattoos, shops and similar cosmetics threaded through Jason and Lucia's story. [S2][S9]
- F10. Pre-orders and purchases made before 20 November 2026 included the Vintage Vice City Pack (retro-styled in-game items) and, for digital pre-orders, one free month of GTA+. [S2][S9]
- F11. The one free GTA+ month had to be redeemed within 180 days of the pre-order and is limited to one per platform account. [S5][S10]
- F12. Prices above are US prices; other regions can differ. [S5]
- F13. Pre-loading opened on 10 November 2026 for digital pre-orders; boxed download-code copies went on sale from 12 November. [S8][S11]
- F14. Storage: Rockstar advised reserving up to 212 GB. [S11]

## Story setup (no spoilers)
- F15. Protagonists are Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a duo with a Bonnie-and-Clyde-style relationship. [S9][S12]
- F16. It is set in the fictional US state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida, centred on Vice City. [S1][S9]
- F17. The official premise: after an easy score goes wrong, Jason and Lucia end up in the middle of a criminal conspiracy across Leonida and must rely on each other. [S1][S3]
- F18. Both Jason and Lucia are central characters; Rockstar frames it as "Jason and Lucia's story". How character switching works is not covered here. [S1][S9]
- F19. You do not need to have played earlier GTA games: it is a new, self-contained story. [S9]
- F20. Rockstar had not published who voices Jason and Lucia as of 10 November 2026. Some supporting cast have been confirmed by the actors themselves. [S9]

## Online and multiplayer
- F21. Rockstar describes Grand Theft Auto VI as a single-player experience. [S5][S10][S13]
- F22. No GTA VI online or multiplayer mode has been announced, and there is no announced date, name or price for one. [S13][S14]
- F23. GTA Online (the multiplayer mode of GTA V) is a separate game and is still running. [S13][S14]
- F24. The one-month GTA+ perk is for GTA Online and the GTA+ library of older games, not for GTA VI multiplayer. [S9]
- F25. PlayStation lists GTA VI as 1 player with offline play enabled. A console subscription is not listed as needed for the story. [S4][S15]

## Ratings and content
- F26. Rated M (Mature 17+) by the ESRB in the US and PEGI 18 in Europe. [S3][S4][S9]
- F27. Rockstar's content descriptors: blood and gore, intense violence, mature humor, nudity, strong language, strong sexual content, use of drugs and alcohol. [S1]

## Performance (as measured by outlets after launch)
- F28. The game targets 30 frames per second on every console. Rockstar told Kotaku that no 40 or 60 fps mode is coming, even on PS5 Pro, because the CPU is the limit. [S16][S17]
- F29. Digital Foundry found a base resolution of 1440p on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X. PS5 Pro upscales to near-4K with PSSR and also gets ray-traced shadows. [S17][S18]
- F30. Digital Foundry ranked PS5 Pro as the best-running version. Series S runs at a much lower resolution and dips more often. [S17][S18]

## Music
- F31. The score is by the French duo Justice. [S9]
- F32. A 34-track soundtrack album ("Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album") released at launch. [S3][S9]
- F33. The in-game radio includes stations such as Cocotea FM (Latin), Back Country Radio (country), AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6 (metal), Flash FM (pop) and Dirty South Classics (rap). [S9]

## Reception
- F34. Take-Two's launch press release calls the game the highest-rated game of all time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S and quotes outlets such as IGN, Eurogamer and The Guardian. That is Take-Two's own claim in a press release. [S3]

## Release history
- F35. Pre-orders opened on 25 June 2026. [S2]
- F36. Trailer 1 was 4 December 2023, Trailer 2 was 6 May 2025, "An Extended Look" was 27 August 2026 and the launch trailer was 15 October 2026. [S1]

## About the game and studio
- F37. Developer and publisher: Rockstar Games (a label of Take-Two Interactive). It is the 17th main GTA game per USA Today. [S1][S3][S9]
- F38. The previous main game, GTA V, came out in 2013, so there was a 13-year gap. [S9]
- F39. The Xbox listing shows HDR10, ray tracing, Dolby Atmos and DTS:X support on Series X|S. Digital Foundry notes Xbox can run the game in a 120Hz VRR container at the OS level, but the game itself is still 30 fps; PlayStation has no 120Hz option. [S10][S18]
